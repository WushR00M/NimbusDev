About Sunrise

Sunrise is a 2D platformer high-speed hack and slash featuring the ability to create and share levels you make to the world, because I wanted to. Made in mostly GameMaker and available on Steam, itch.io, and coming soon to XBOX, it's probably my most ambitious project yet.

About GMLua

This is a library and extension for GameMaker that adds Lua support into the game. It's a bit primitive at the moment and I'm currently out of time (thank you Sunrise) to be actively developing it at this moment.

Feel free to fork the source and make something even cooler out of it, as long as it's free!

About IMDM?

Old utility project of mine that acts as a survey to see if a device is moddable. It's outdated, so don't rely on it, especially in the section related to XBOX 360 as they've had some huge developments since the last update.
